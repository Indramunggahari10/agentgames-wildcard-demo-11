Steal-the-VFY Turn Checker

Validate a turn response against the published Steal the VFY protocol before it becomes a HOLD. Paste the expected matchId and turn from the signed turn envelope, paste the response, and get a per-rule checks report.

Honest scope. Schema/shape validation only. No live data, no proofs generated or verified, no publicInputsHash computed, no platform calls, nothing stored. A “valid” verdict means every documented shape rule passed — the platform still verifies the proof and computes its own hash independently.

The amount rule — read this first. For HOLD or FORTIFY, the amount field must be omitted entirely: the key must not be present at all. The schema rejects the field even when 0. Sending amount: 0 or amount: null is still an error — delete the key, do not zero it. (SPEC.md §4.)
